TeslaBatteryBLE tracks your Tesla's battery locally over Bluetooth LE. Scan for nearby Teslas, pair a vehicle key with an NFC card tap on the console, and read battery state of charge and charge state over an encrypted session. A foreground service keeps SOC tracking alive while parked or charging, with an on-device history graph (6 h / 24 h / 7 d / all) and since-last-charge stats. The car is only woken when you ask.

Privacy first: the app has no internet permission, needs no account, and never sends anything itself. Keys stay in app-private storage and are device-only by default (encrypted with this device's Keystore, excluded from backup). You can opt in to include the key in encrypted Android backup on Android 12+ so pairing survives a phone change; older versions keep the key out of backup. The charging-manager scoped key can read and control charging, but cannot unlock or drive. Battery and connection history stay in a local database, and may be included in your device's own system backup if you have it enabled. Uninstalling removes on-device data.

Requirements: Android 8.0+ and a Tesla with BLE key support. Pairing needs physical access to the car (NFC key card tap plus on-screen vehicle confirmation).

Status: beta. Preview APKs are debug builds from GitHub Releases. Signed stable releases and F-Droid distribution are planned.
