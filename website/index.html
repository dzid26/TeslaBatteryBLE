Features

  • Scan & pair

    Find nearby Teslas by advertised BLE name and pair a key with an NFC card tap on the console.

  • Encrypted session

    Read battery SOC and charge state over an authenticated, encrypted BLE session.

  • On-demand wake

    The car is only woken when you ask — never in the background.

  • Background tracking

    A foreground service keeps the connection alive while parked or charging, updating the SOC timeline.

  • SOC history graph

    View 6 h / 24 h / 7 d / all-time charge history with since-last-charge stats.

  • Simulated car

    Debug builds include a simulated vehicle for development and screenshots without a real car.

Privacy

TeslaBatteryBLE is local-first. The app has no internet permission and sends nothing anywhere.

  • Vehicle keys stay in device-only app storage by default.
  • Opt-in encrypted Android backup is available in Settings; on Android 12+ it requires your device lock secret.
  • Known cars, SOC history, and settings live in a local database on your phone.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, ads, accounts, or telemetry.

Install

GitHub Releases (preview)

  1. Go to the rolling preview release.
  2. Download the latest APK.
  3. Open the APK on your Android device and allow installation from the browser/file manager.

Preview APKs are debug builds. They install side by side with future stable releases.

Obtainium (auto-updates)

  1. Open Obtainium and tap Add App.
  2. Enter https://github.com/dzid26/TeslaBatteryBLE.
  3. Enable Include prereleases (the preview channel is a prerelease).
  4. Install and let Obtainium track new previews.

Status & roadmap

TeslaBatteryBLE is in beta. The rolling preview is rebuilt from main on every push. Signed stable releases and F-Droid are on the roadmap.
