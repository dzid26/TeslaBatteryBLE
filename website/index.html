TeslaBatteryBLE

An Android app that talks to a Tesla over Bluetooth LE: battery state, charge state, and a local history. Keys are enrolled on the car; there is no account and no server.

Features

  • Scanning and pairing

    Finds cars by their advertised BLE name and enrolls a key with an NFC card tap on the console.

  • Sessions

    Battery and charge data are read over the protocol's authenticated, encrypted session.

  • Waking

    The car is woken only on request, not in the background.

  • Background tracking

    A foreground service keeps the connection and the SOC timeline updated while parked or charging.

  • History

    6 h / 24 h / 7 d / all-time views with since-last-charge stats.

  • Simulated car

    Debug builds include a simulated vehicle, so development and screenshots don't need a car.

Privacy

The app has no internet permission; it only talks to the car over Bluetooth.

  • Vehicle keys are kept in app-private storage; by default they are encrypted with the device Keystore and left out of backups.
  • Including the keys in Android backup is opt-in in Settings; on Android 12+ it requires encrypted backup.
  • Known cars, SOC history, and settings are stored in a local database.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or telemetry.

The full privacy statement is in the repository.

Install

GitHub Releases (preview)

  1. Go to the rolling preview release.
  2. Download the latest APK.
  3. Open the APK on your Android device and allow installation from the browser/file manager.

Preview APKs are debug builds. They install side by side with future stable releases.

Obtainium (auto-updates)

  1. Open Obtainium and tap Add App.
  2. Enter https://github.com/dzid26/TeslaBatteryBLE.
  3. Enable Include prereleases (the preview channel is a prerelease).
  4. Install and let Obtainium track new previews.

Status & roadmap

The app is in beta. The rolling preview release is rebuilt from main on every push; signed stable releases and F-Droid are planned.
