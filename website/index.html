TeslaBatteryBLE

An Android app that talks to a Tesla over Bluetooth LE: battery state, charge state, and a local history. Pair it with your car — the keys stay safely on your phone. No dongle or extra hardware — just your smartphone.

Install

TeslaBatteryBLE is in beta. Every release so far is a pre-release, cut from main.

GitHub Releases (preview APK)

  1. Open the releases page and pick the newest release.
  2. Download the APK attached to the release.
  3. Open the APK on your Android device and allow installation from the browser or file manager.

Preview APKs are debug builds, signed with the same key as releases and sharing their app ID, so a later release installs over a preview in place.

Obtainium (auto-updates)

  1. Open Obtainium and tap Add App.
  2. Enter https://github.com/dzid26/TeslaBatteryBLE.
  3. Obtainium tracks stable releases by default; enable Include prereleases to get the pre-release builds.
  4. Install and let Obtainium keep it updated.

No stable release has shipped yet, so pre-releases are currently the only channel.

Privacy

The app has no internet permission; it only talks to the car over Bluetooth, and keys and history stay on the device. The full privacy statement is in the repository.
